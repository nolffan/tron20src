# playtest-recall.txt -- the disc's recall (the right mouse button,
# "Defend") in the first level, M01-01. Into control (skipcine answers the
# decision window); the cheat mpkfa (all weapons); 1 opens the weapon
# chooser and a click of the fire button takes its first weapon, the disc.
# The view is the start's: the far wall in front, the bright floor below.
# The disc is thrown (left click) at the far wall, which it hits about
# 0.75 s later; without a recall it then returns, about 0.9 s after that.
#   1. a short right click 0.3 s after the throw: the disc is back 0.85 s
#      later, before it could hit the wall and come back by itself;
#   2. the right button held (0.6 s) from 0.3 s after the throw: the same;
#   3. no recall: at the same time the disc is not back yet; it comes back
#      after it hits the wall (the server's log: the recall's disc is back
#      0.75 s after the throw, the other 1.6 s after it).
# `disc` checks the lower middle of the view, where the held disc is drawn
# (build/playtest.sh). Our DISCCLASSDATA::Init read "ReturnToOwner" with
# CButeMgr::GetByte where retail calls GetBool: the value ("true") was a
# type mismatch, 0, so no disc returned to its owner. A recall did nothing,
# and a disc that hit a wall was lost (docs/PLAN.md, section 7). Run it on
# the legacy and the modern build, and on the modern build with
# TEST_CFG=build/rawmouse-0.cfg (DirectInput's mouse, not raw input).
# world: Worlds\RetailSinglePlayer\M01-01
window 240
skipcine 180
key t
wait 0.5
type mpkfa
key Return
wait 2
key 1
wait 1
click 1 0.1
wait 2
disc ready in
# 1. a short right click
click 1 0.1
wait 0.2
disc thrown-1 out
click 3 0.1
wait 0.75
disc recalled-short in
wait 2
# 2. the right button held
click 1 0.1
wait 0.2
disc thrown-2 out
click 3 0.6
wait 0.25
disc recalled-held in
wait 2
# 3. no recall: the disc comes back by itself after it hits the wall
click 1 0.1
wait 0.2
disc thrown-3 out
wait 0.85
disc not-back-yet out
wait 3
disc returned in
