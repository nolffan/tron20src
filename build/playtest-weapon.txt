# playtest-weapon.txt -- a quick load while the weapon chooser is open,
# in the first single-player level. Into control (skipcine answers the
# decision window "Continue basic training?"); a quicksave (F5); the key 1
# (it opens the weapon chooser); a short move; a quickload (F9, Yes) while
# the chooser is still open; back into control; 1 again.
# The load exits the world: CInterfaceMgr::OnExitWorld takes the HUD items
# away (CHUDWeapon::Term sets g_pHUDWeapon to NULL), then closes the open
# chooser, and CTronWeaponChooser::Close (0x100afde0) gives the weapon to
# the HUD's weapon item. Retail reads g_pWeaponItem there (the HUD
# manager's pointer, which stays set). Our Close read g_pHUDWeapon, and
# the legacy and modern builds crashed in CHUDWeapon::SetWeapon (a read
# at address 0x18). Retail's DLLs (make control) do not crash.
# world: Worlds\RetailSinglePlayer\M01-01
window 240
skipcine 180
shot start
key F5
wait 4
shot quicksaved
key 1
wait 0.5
shot chooser
hold s 0.3
key F9
wait 3
shot quickload-asked
# "This will end your current game. Proceed?": Yes
mclick 292 256
wait 20
nohud quickload
skipcine 90
shot quickloaded
key 1
wait 0.5
key 1
wait 3
shot weapon
hold w 0.5
wait 1
shot after-load
